### Context

Reliable telecommunications are an enabling layer for grid protection, automation, operational coordination, and restoration. Modernisation programmes need to balance lifecycle management, interoperability, service continuity, and security.

### Professional contribution

In my utility role, I have supported telecom systems and modernisation activities across transmission environments. Relevant technology areas include fibre and OPGW, microwave, SDH/PDH, MPLS-TP, PLCC, teleprotection interfaces, time synchronisation, and network-security controls.

### Research relevance

This experience motivates research into dependable cyber-physical infrastructure, communication resilience, secure migration of legacy systems, and methods for assessing the impact of communication failures or cyber incidents on critical services.
