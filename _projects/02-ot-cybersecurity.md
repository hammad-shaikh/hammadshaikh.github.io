### Motivation

Electric utilities depend on dependable communications between substations, control centres, protection systems, and operational technology. Security improvements must account for availability, safety, legacy equipment, interoperability, and operational constraints.

### Applied experience

My professional experience at K-Electric has included transmission telecom systems and OT-facing environments, including fibre-optic communications, SDH/MPLS-TP, microwave, power-line carrier communication (PLCC), teleprotection interfaces, and substation/control-centre communication contexts.

My research interest is how cybersecurity principles can be translated into practical and measurable improvements for critical infrastructure, informed by standards and frameworks such as **IEC 62443** and relevant NIST guidance.

### Potential research directions

- Risk-based security architecture for utility OT networks
- Segmentation and secure remote-access patterns for industrial environments
- Monitoring and incident response that respect operational constraints
- Resilience evaluation for communication-dependent protection and control
- Secure-by-design integration of legacy and modern utility systems
