I contribute to technical learning and development through internal training and mentoring. Topics have included project management, data analytics and reporting, Power BI, and technical communication. I have also supported postgraduate teaching in information privacy and security.

### Teaching interests

- Information privacy and security fundamentals
- Differential privacy and privacy-preserving data sharing
- OT/ICS cybersecurity and critical-infrastructure security
- Technical project delivery and engineering communication

### For academic collaboration

I am interested in helping develop practical learning material, case studies, and research-informed exercises that connect cybersecurity concepts with real engineering constraints. Any case study derived from industry work should be anonymised and approved for public use.
