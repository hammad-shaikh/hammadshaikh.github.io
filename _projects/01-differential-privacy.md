### Research question

How can privacy-preserving mechanisms support trustworthy data sharing while making the privacy–utility trade-off explicit?

### Overview

My master's thesis, **“Analysis of Privacy-Preserving Techniques and Algorithms for Trustworthy Data Sharing,”** studied differential-privacy approaches for releasing or analysing data while limiting disclosure about individuals. The work considered the Laplace, Gaussian, and Exponential mechanisms and explored different privacy-budget settings.

### Methods and topics

- Differential privacy and privacy-budget (\u03b5) choices
- Laplace, Gaussian, and Exponential mechanisms
- Privacy–utility trade-offs and sensitivity
- Trustworthy data sharing and responsible analytics

### Next research questions

Potential extensions include evaluating privacy–utility trade-offs on additional datasets, improving experimental reproducibility, and studying privacy-preserving analytics for sensitive operational or infrastructure data.
