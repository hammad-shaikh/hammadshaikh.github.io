## Suggested CV order for PhD applications

1. Research profile and interests
2. Education and thesis
3. Research outputs and publications (distinguish peer-reviewed work from thesis/preprints)
4. Research and technical projects
5. Professional experience relevant to the proposed PhD
6. Teaching, mentoring, and leadership
7. Selected certifications and professional affiliations
8. Technical skills
9. References (or “available on request,” if appropriate)

Keep the CV evidence-led and tailored to each doctoral project. Do not list a certification or achievement unless it is completed and verifiable.
